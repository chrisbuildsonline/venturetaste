## The idea

- **pitch:** An autonomous 4-agent M&A and venture diligence pipeline that leverages Qloo's 250M entity graph to deterministically quantify brand synergy, customer retention overlap, and cultural churn risk for consumer brand acquisitions.
- **why it wins:** Appeals directly to investor judge Jason Calacanis by elevating Qloo from consumer recommendations to high-margin commercial due diligence, combining cross-domain affinity data with deterministic risk scorecards.
- **demo moment:** An investor inputs target acquisition brand pairs; within 20 seconds, VentureTaste queries Qloo cross-domain affinities across fashion, hospitality, and media, computes a deterministic Brand Cannibalization Index (BCI), and produces an interactive radar chart revealing a 68% subcultural audience clash missed by standard spreadsheets.
- **stack:** Python, FastAPI, Qloo Taste AI API, LangGraph, Pydantic, Next.js, Tailwind, Recharts.
- **build days:** 4
